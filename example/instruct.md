## Implementing a general pebble-like ripa scheduler.
You can first look at [aod_vs_ripa.md](/doc/aod_vs_ripa.md) for an overview

Now you may notice that [defect_free_assembly.py](example/defect_free_assembly.py) and [aod_defect_free_assembly](example/aod_defect_free_assembly.py) is not a fare comparison between aod and ripa, since ripa runs on a less dense grid, that allow the exsistence of "highway", that allow one to transport atoms without obstacle. You may look [pebble_problem.md](doc/pebble_problem.md) for information. However, now i want you to adapt [ripa_pebble.py](src/scheduler/ripa_pebble.py) to a new RIPAPebbleAdv1Scheduler class, that does not take in the highway_period, auxiliary_lane_penalty, stuff, just figure out the scheduling **completely from its geometry**, just as in the original pebble problem. I assume some searching algorithm with some heuristic would help. For example check [Continuous-CBS](/tmp/Continuous-CBS/)

Test your RIPAPebbleAdv1Scheduler by copying [defect_free_assembly_adv.py](example/defect_free_assembly_adv.py) to defect_free_assembly_adv1.py and test the new algorithm. Work in conda vipa env.
